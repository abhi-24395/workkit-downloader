# WorkKit Downloader

One download manager for everything you save, on Mac, Windows and Linux: files of any size, videos and
music from YouTube, SoundCloud and over a thousand other sites, and images. Big files download faster over
parallel connections and resume where they stopped; videos come in the quality you choose, as MP4s that
play everywhere, or as just the audio.

Product page, pricing and install guide: **https://www.workkit.in/downloader/**

This repository hosts the release downloads. The app is not open source.

## Download (1.1)

Get the files from the [latest release](https://github.com/abhi-24395/workkit-downloader/releases/latest).

| Computer | File |
|---|---|
| Mac: macOS 12 or later, Apple silicon (M1 or newer) | `WorkKitDownloader-1.1.dmg` |
| Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit | `WorkKitDownloader-Setup.exe`, or `WorkKitDownloader-windows.zip` (portable) |
| Linux x86_64 (Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 36 or newer) | `WorkKitDownloader-linux-x86_64-1.1.tar.gz` |
| Browser extension (optional; Chrome, Edge, Brave) | `WorkKitDownloader-Chrome-extension-1.1.zip` |

The app is signed with WorkKit's own certificate, not Apple's or Microsoft's paid ones, so the first launch
shows a one-time warning. The [install guide](https://www.workkit.in/downloader/#install) walks through it.
Each release lists SHA-256 checksums of its files.

## Price

Details: https://www.workkit.in/downloader/#pricing

## Not supported

DRM-protected streaming services and live streams while they are live. Download only what you have the
right to keep; many sites' terms, including YouTube's, restrict downloading.

## Privacy

No account, no analytics, no telemetry. Downloads go straight from the website to your computer and the
license key is checked offline. Signing in to sites is optional, off by default, and uses your browser's
sign-in only on your own computer. Full list: https://www.workkit.in/downloader/#privacy
